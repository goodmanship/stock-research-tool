Why the investor bought — what the evidence supports

Berkshire is accumulating LEN, but we did not verify its stock-specific homebuilding thesis. The housing-shortage/recovery interpretation is ours. GRBK is already a 19.39% unchanged Greenlight position; Einhorn co-founded the business, and the Q2 letter lists it among the largest longs. That affiliation and long-standing ownership are not evidence of fresh open-market buying or a disclosed fair value.

Our thesis · analogue of LEN

Housing demand and disciplined land economics in a smaller builder. GRBK's $2.82B cap is roughly one-sixth of LEN's $18.15B; vendor forward P/E is 10.9x versus 14.4x, ROE 16.4% versus 6.0%, and D/E 0.14 versus 0.28. These are screening comparisons, not proof of superior through-cycle returns.

Valuation cross-check

$65.47 is about 1.46x company Q2 book value of $44.82 per share. Q2 homebuilding gross margin was 29.8% and net homebuilding debt/capital 6.1%. Vendor FCF yield is just 0.25%; 2025 FCF was $208.4M, but 2024 only $21.5M because land/inventory spending moves cash flow. A three-year average gives 5.2% at today's market cap, not a normalized forecast. A 25% forecast EPS haircut gives 14.6x.

Where the thesis or analogy breaks

Texas concentration, rate-sensitive affordability and owned-land/inventory exposure. It does not copy LEN's asset-light strategy. Q2 incentives were 9%, gross margin fell 150 basis points year over year, and backlog dollars fell 23.6% despite stronger orders.

What must be established next

Stress lower selling prices and land write-downs before relying on book value. Check land investment returns and whether improved orders become profitable deliveries without larger incentives.

Executive summary

Green Brick Partners, Inc.

operates as a diversified homebuilding and land development company in the United States.

The company engages in the homebuilding process, which includes acquiring and developing land, securing entitlements, designing homes, constructing properties, providing title, mortgage, and insurance agency services, and managing marketing and sales, as well as creating master-planned communities.

It also provides financial services platforms, including Green Brick Title, GRBK Mortgage, and Green Brick Insurance.
