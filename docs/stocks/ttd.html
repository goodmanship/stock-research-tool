Why the investor bought — what the evidence supports

Translate the digital-advertising and scalable-platform part of Alphabet's economics, not its search moat. Buffett's actual framework is durable high returns; we have not established that TTD meets it.

Our thesis · analogue of GOOGL

An independent buy-side advertising platform with data integrations and high retention. $5.66B cap, 11.9x vendor forward earnings, 15.4% ROE and 10.3% vendor FCF yield are attractive screening numbers against Alphabet's much larger size and 22.9x forward multiple.

Valuation cross-check

2025 annual FCF was $783.0M (13.8% at today's cap), but $490.6M stock compensation reduces a rough owner-cash proxy to $292.3M (5.2%). H1 2026 operating cash less property/software spending was $412.0M; subtracting $218.6M stock compensation leaves $193.4M, with working capital still affecting cash. This is a dilution-cost sensitivity, not an audited owner-earnings definition.

Where the thesis or analogy breaks

Q2 revenue grew only 3%, net income fell and adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 34% from 39%. Amazon/Google competition, identity/privacy rules and execution threaten the economics. TTD buys ads across third-party inventory; it does not own search intent, YouTube or Cloud.

What must be established next

Verify growth stabilization, take-rate durability and cash after dilution. Keep on the conditional list; smaller/cheaper is established, comparable moat quality is not.

Executive summary

The Trade Desk, Inc.

operates as a technology company in the United States and internationally.

The company creates, manages, and optimizes digital advertising campaigns across ad formats, channels and devices, including CTV and other video, display, audio, and native, on a multitude of devices, such televisions, streaming devices, mobile devices, computers and digital-out-of-home devices.

It provides data and other value-added services.
