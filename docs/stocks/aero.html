Executive summary

Grupo Aeroméxico, S.A.B.

de C.V., through its subsidiaries, provide public air carrier services for passengers and goods.

It offers scheduled passenger air carrier services, cargo air carrier services, and other services.

The company also offers loyalty program management, training, franchise systems, and investment management services.

Business model and why it may be working

  • Makes money by selling passenger tickets, cargo capacity, and loyalty-program economics across its route network.
  • Recent growth is being helped by revenue expanding about 12.6% year over year.
  • Its edge is less obvious from the raw numbers alone, so this one deserves a more qualitative look at competitive position and management execution.
