Yahoo's 504× trailing multiple should not be treated as representative normalized earnings. Company Q2 guidance is $7.50–$8.00 adjusted EPS, implying 15.8–16.8× at this price, and $550–$700 million FCF, implying roughly 3.2–4.0% on $17.38 billion market cap. The automotive/industrial separation is planned for Q1 2027. Research priority, not a clean bargain based on the automatic Strong Buy label.
  • HCC — Pabrai conviction, but not fresh accumulation. It is 43.32% of his disclosed portfolio, yet he trimmed 3.69% in Q2, after a 0.61% Q1 add. Negative vendor FCF and commodity cyclicality make current earnings an unreliable long-run baseline. Normalize metallurgical coal prices, expansion spending, and production before valuing it.
